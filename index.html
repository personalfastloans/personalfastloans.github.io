What is a personal loan?

A personal loan is a lump sum you repay on a fixed schedule, usually with a fixed interest rate. People use them for home repairs, medical bills, moving costs, car repairs and consolidating higher-interest debt. Because the payment is the same each month, it is easier to fit into a budget than a balance that keeps changing.

Speedy Funds Now is a loan connection service, not a lender. You complete one secure form, the service matches your request with lenders in its network, and you review any offers in full before deciding. Approval, rates and terms are decided by each lender.

Four loan types and who each one suits

Each type solves a different problem. Match the loan to the need, then compare the total cost, not only the monthly payment.

Personal loans in USA

Larger or longer-term needs with fixed monthly payments: repairs, medical costs, moving or debt consolidation. Usually the most affordable option if you can handle a multi-month term.

Best for: planned or larger expenses.

Installment loan options

Repaid in equal installments over a set term, so every payment and the end date are known on day one. Useful when a single repayment date would be too tight.

Best for: people who want predictable payments.

No credit check loans

Lenders in this category weigh current income and employment more heavily than credit history. Costs are often higher, so read the full terms with extra care.

Best for: limited or damaged credit with steady income.

Payday loans online

A small, short-term advance usually repaid in full on your next pay date. It is the most expensive way to borrow, so treat it as a last resort for a genuine short-term gap.

Best for: small urgent amounts you can repay in one payment.

Borrow responsibly. Only borrow what you can repay on schedule. Late or missed payments can bring extra fees and may hurt your credit. If you cannot repay a loan, contact the lender before the due date.

Seven checks before you accept any loan

  • Confirm the lender is licensed to lend in your state.
  • Read the APR, not only the monthly payment.
  • Add up every fee: origination, late and returned-payment charges.
  • Ask whether early repayment carries a penalty.
  • Check the total you will repay over the full term.
  • Ask whether the lender runs a soft or hard credit inquiry.
  • Never pay an upfront fee to "guarantee" approval. That is a common scam sign.

Frequently asked questions

What is a personal loan?

A personal loan is money you borrow once and repay in fixed installments over several months or years. People use it for repairs, medical bills, moving costs or to consolidate debt.

How much can I borrow?

Lenders in the Speedy Funds Now network offer $100 to $35,000. Your amount depends on your income, the lender's criteria and your state's regulations.

Is Speedy Funds Now a lender?

No. It is a loan connection service. It does not make credit decisions or set terms. Each lender discloses its own APR, fees and repayment schedule before you sign.

Will checking my offers affect my credit score?

That depends on the lender, as some use soft inquiries and others use hard inquiries. Check the terms before you accept.

Can I get a loan with bad credit?

Some lenders focus on income rather than credit history. See no credit check loan options, and compare the total cost carefully because rates are often higher.

How fast will I receive the money?

Once you accept an offer and sign, the lender deposits funds to your bank account. Timing varies by lender and bank processing speed, so confirm it in your agreement.
